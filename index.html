The Ritual of Oud

For centuries, oud has been more than fragrance. It is ceremony. It is memory. It is the invisible thread connecting the mortal to the eternal.

In the palaces of Arabia, in the courts of the Mughal emperors, in the sanctuaries of Southeast Asia — oud has always marked the sacred moments of human existence.

01

Apply on Pulse Points

The warmth of your skin awakens the oud, releasing its deepest notes slowly over time.

02

Allow the Drydown

Oud reveals itself in layers. The first hour is merely the opening chapter.

03

Never Rub the Skin

Let the essence rest. Friction disturbs the molecular structure and diminishes the full depth.
