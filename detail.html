Oud is not worn — it is invited. Each application is a ceremony. Follow the ritual of the ancients to fully honour this essence.

01

Warm the Skin

Apply immediately after bathing. Clean, warm skin is the finest canvas for oud. The heat opens the pores and draws the oil deeper.

02

Pulse Points Only

Inner wrists, behind the ears, the base of the throat. Never more than three points — oud is potent by nature.

03

Do Not Rub

Touch the applicator to the skin and let it rest. Friction breaks the molecular structure and shortens the journey of the scent.

04

Allow the Drydown

The first chapter of an oud is merely the opening. Wait an hour before passing judgment. The truest character reveals itself slowly.

Agarwood — the sacred heartwood of Aquilaria trees — forms only when the tree responds to infection or injury. This wound-born resin, accumulated over decades, is the rarest organic substance on earth.
